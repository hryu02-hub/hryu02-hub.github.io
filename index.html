About

I am interested in robot learning, particularly in developing scalable and data-efficient methods for learning complex robotic behaviors. I am especially interested in leveraging structural and physical inductive biases, including insights from control and dynamics, to improve how robots learn and physically interact with their environments. My interests also extend to deep reinforcement learning and computer vision for increasingly dexterous and general robotic systems.

Selected Research

Visuomotor Robot Learning

Robot Learning · Manipulation · Geometric Representations

Exploring visuomotor policies and geometric representations for robotic manipulation, with a current focus on learning robust behaviors under physical interaction and contact uncertainty.

Active Perception and 3D Representation

Computer Vision · Active Perception · 3D Geometry

Worked on perception pipelines involving 3D scene representation and viewpoint selection for robotic systems, including experimental evaluation with real robots.
