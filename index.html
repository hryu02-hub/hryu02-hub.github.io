I am a senior undergraduate student in Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science at Seoul National University. I am currently conducting my B.S. thesis research in the Robotics Laboratory with Prof. Frank C. Park. Previously, I worked on robot perception for tabletop cluttered scenes in the RPM Robotics Lab with Prof. Ayoung Kim.

I am interested in robot learning, particularly in developing scalable and data-efficient methods for learning complex robotic behaviors. I am especially interested in leveraging structural and physical inductive biases, including insights from control and dynamics, to improve how robots learn and physically interact with their environments. My interests also extend to deep reinforcement learning and computer vision for increasingly dexterous and general robotic systems.

Robotics Laboratory · Seoul National University

Geometric Visuomotor Learning for Contact-Rich Manipulation

Ongoing B.S. Thesis Work

I study how geometric representations and feedback mechanisms can improve the robustness and spatial generalization of learned visuomotor policies for contact-rich manipulation. My current work explores how learned policies can be structured and combined with force feedback and compliant robot control during physical interaction.

I am developing simulation and evaluation pipelines to study policy representations under spatial transformations and the interaction between learned visuomotor policies and low-level feedback control.

RPM Robotics Lab · Seoul National University

Perception and Spatial Reasoning for Post-Grasp Retrieval in Clutter

I worked on perception and spatial reasoning for post-grasp retrieval in cluttered tabletop scenes, where a robot must extract a target object while minimizing disturbance to surrounding objects.

My work included implementing and evaluating a TSDF-based active perception baseline, exploring TSDF and 3D Gaussian Splatting for 3D scene representation, developing a VLM-based pipeline for reasoning about retrieval directions from visual observations, and building the real-robot evaluation pipeline and quantitative metrics for measuring clutter disturbance.

Interactive and Networked Robotics Laboratory · Seoul National University

Contact Dynamics Simulation with Cascaded Newton-Augmented Lagrangian Methods

Developed a MATLAB simulator for rigid-body contact dynamics using cascaded Newton based augmented-Lagrangian numerical methods. The project investigated numerical formulations for resolving unilateral contact constraints and provided a simulation testbed for studying physically grounded robot dynamics and control.

Circa — Noninvasive Sleep Monitoring and Environment-Adaptive IoT Hub

Developed a contactless sleep-monitoring system using an 8 × 8 time-of-flight LiDAR array and multimodal environmental sensors. The system combined noninvasive bedside sensing with environment-aware IoT functionality for monitoring sleep and surrounding conditions.
