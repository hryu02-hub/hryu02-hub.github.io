Overview

As part of my research experience in the Interactive and Networked Robotics Laboratory at Seoul National University, I studied numerical methods for rigid-body contact dynamics and implemented a MATLAB version of the Cascaded Newton-based Augmented Lagrangian (CANAL) contact solver.

The goal of the project was to understand the numerical structure behind modern multi-contact simulation by reproducing the core solver pipeline and validating it on simplified rigid-contact scenarios.

What I Implemented

Contact Dynamics Formulation

Implemented discrete rigid-body dynamics with unilateral contact constraints and frictional contact variables.

Augmented-Lagrangian Iteration

Implemented the outer augmented-Lagrangian updates used to iteratively enforce consistency between system dynamics and contact constraints.

Cascaded Newton Solver

Reproduced the cascaded Newton structure used by CANAL, including Newton updates for the inner surrogate problem and line-search-based step selection.

Simulation Validation

Built MATLAB simulation cases to examine contact behavior, solver convergence, and the numerical interaction between rigid-body dynamics and contact impulses.

Outcome

This project gave me hands-on experience with nonlinear contact dynamics, complementarity-based modeling, augmented-Lagrangian optimization, Newton methods, and the practical numerical issues involved in implementing a physics simulator from mathematical formulations.

The implementation served primarily as a technical reproduction and learning project rather than a new contact-solver contribution.

Reference
