Overview

Circa is a bedside mechatronics system designed to monitor sleep without requiring wearable sensors while also sensing and controlling the surrounding sleep environment.

The system combines an 8 × 8 time-of-flight LiDAR array, acoustic sensing, and environmental sensors to estimate breathing and body movement, detect abnormal sleep-related events, characterize the surrounding environment, and provide IoT-based environmental control.

Contactless Sleep Monitoring

The primary sensing modality is an 8 × 8 ToF LiDAR array positioned near the bed. Small changes in measured distance capture periodic chest motion from respiration, while larger spatial changes provide information about body movement and tossing and turning.

Respiration Sensing

Processed ToF distance measurements using temporal filtering to suppress sensor noise and extract slow periodic displacement associated with breathing.

Breathing Rate

Extracted respiratory cycles from the filtered signal and estimated breathing-rate statistics over time.

Apnea Detection

Monitored prolonged disappearance of respiratory oscillations to detect potential abnormal breathing events during sleep.

Body Movement

Used changes across the ToF array to distinguish respiration-scale motion from larger tossing, turning, and wake-related movement.

Event Detection

The signal-processing pipeline was designed to recover low-amplitude respiratory motion from noisy ToF measurements while retaining larger body-motion events as a separate cue.

Sleep-State Estimation

Circa combines respiration and movement-related metrics to characterize changes in sleep state. Features include estimated breaths per minute, respiratory variability, movement energy, and the frequency of tossing and turning.

Rather than relying on a single sensor measurement, the system uses multiple behavioral signals to distinguish relatively stable deep-sleep periods from more variable sleep or waking conditions.

Environmental Awareness

Sleep quality depends not only on the sleeper but also on the surrounding environment. Circa therefore combines sleep sensing with multimodal environmental monitoring.

Acoustic Context

Used sound sensing and a lightweight classification pipeline to distinguish different acoustic conditions, including human chatter and traffic-like environmental noise.

Snoring Cue

Acoustic information provides an additional sleep-related cue alongside the ToF-based respiration and motion signals.

Ambient Conditions

Monitored environmental variables including temperature, humidity, ambient noise, and light level.

Light Sensing

Integrated ambient-light measurements into the bedside sensing system to track lighting conditions during sleep.

Environment-Adaptive IoT Hub

Beyond passive monitoring, Circa was designed as a bedside IoT hub that connects sensed sleep and environmental conditions to controllable devices in the room.

The system integrated remote-control functionality for environmental devices, allowing sensing, sleep monitoring, and room-control functions to operate through a single embedded platform.

Outcome

Circa integrated sensing, signal processing, embedded computation, simple machine learning, and IoT control into a single working mechatronics system.

The project was awarded 1st Place in the SNU Mechatronics Design Competition.
